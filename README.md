Hello traveller,this is the project Starbie 
It is a tiny motion-controlled digital pet: basically a desktop Tamagotchi with minimal gameplay buttons. Instead of pressing controls, you interact with it by physically moving the board.
Basically supposed to act like a digital pet with a physical body
I made it as a part of the first week of half-life since I didn't know how to use KiCad at all
My Starbie, who I shall refer to as Dusk is different from the og in the aspect that it has some LEDs.
Its also incredibly well drawn (sarcasm)
